category: Changed

The relay contract is now relay v13: it says who runs the closing note and the finish step. An orchestrator that is a program may run both itself, after `VERDICT: PASS`, taking the note text from the `Outcome:` line of the implementer's last report and recording itself as the actor with `--owner`; the implementer then writes `Outcome:` (one line) in its report and runs neither command, and still runs both when the orchestrator is an agent. `slicer ai relay --json` and `slicer ai relay-orchestrator --json` carry the rule as the `finish_runner` conformance obligation after `finish_after_pass`, and Order's step 3 and the implementer's role say the same. A project pinned to relay v12 is refused the v13 text with the existing pin message.
