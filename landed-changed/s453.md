category: Changed

The relay contract is v12: every role, the read-only ones included, may end its report with up to 3 tool feedback lines about slicer or another tool, and the orchestrator carries them into its run report. The run report gains a `tool feedback` line per entry before `role steps` (`tool feedback: N. ROLE | tool: slicer|ork|NAME | kind: friction|feature|deterministic-check | proposal: TEXT | evidence: TEXT`, `tool feedback: none` when empty, at most 3, counted toward the 30 lines, `evidence` last), `report.lines` gains a `tool_feedback` entry before `role_steps`, and `ai relay --json` and `ai relay-orchestrator --json` gain a `tool_feedback` key (`max_entries`, `kinds`, `fields`) after `conformance` (before `session` under `--session`). The built-in `relay-brief.md` Rules gain a bullet that tells the orchestrator to carry the feedback; a project's customized copy keeps its own text until it adds the bullet. Tool feedback is advisory: it is not a finding, is never gated and is never acted on in the slice, and it does not use `slicer feedback` (s453).
